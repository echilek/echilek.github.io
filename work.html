  4. 04

    Needle-Nose Pliers — CAD & Structural Simulation

    Coursework · SolidWorks · Static FEA · Modal analysis

    Modeled and assembled a set of needle-nose pliers in SolidWorks from a provided engineering drawing, then evaluated the assembly using static and frequency studies. A 40 psi handle load identified stress concentration near the pivot region, while modal analysis characterized the assembly’s natural frequencies and mode shapes.

  5. 05

    Custom Pine Desk — Design & Fabrication

    High-school Woodshop II · Woodworking · Joinery · Fabrication

    Designed and built a custom pine desk over one semester in high-school Woodshop II, using mortise-and-tenon and dado joinery for the primary structure. The design incorporated a pull-out keyboard tray, standard drawer, removable upper shelving, and an adjustable shelf within the material and tooling constraints of the school woodshop.

  6. 06

    Small-Scale Motorjet — First Iteration

    Personal development project · System design · Sheet metal · Prototyping

    Personal project to develop a small-scale motorjet around an electric ducted fan, with a custom casing, combustion chamber, and electrical and ignition layouts. Electrical and ignition subsystems were verified independently, but the first iteration stopped when thin-sheet fabrication prevented reliable structural integration.
