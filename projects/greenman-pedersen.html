Overview

I completed two summer internships at Greenman-Pedersen, Inc., progressing from mechanical and plumbing drafting into HVAC design responsibilities. During my second summer, I was given substantially more ownership of individual design assignments, including equipment selection, system layouts, sizing, and drawing production under the direction of senior engineers.

Summer 2025 — Documentation & Existing Conditions

I drafted mechanical and plumbing systems in Revit and AutoCAD, updated large existing models using as-built drawings, and created equipment and system schedules. Detail views and drawing documentation were a substantial part of this work.

Summer 2026 — HVAC Design

During my second summer, I took on more HVAC design responsibilities, including heating and cooling load calculations, equipment selection, duct layout and sizing, and grille, register, and diffuser (GRD) selection. I also produced mechanical drawings, reviewed submittals, and prepared first-pass responses to RFIs.

Featured Project — Army Office / Storage Facility HVAC Redesign

One of my largest assignments was the HVAC redesign of an Army office and storage facility. I was responsible for laying out and selecting fan-coil units for most rooms, developing and sizing ductwork, selecting and placing GRDs, and preparing the mechanical drawings for the 60% Design Development package.

The design was developed under applicable Unified Facilities Criteria (UFC) requirements and submitted for Army engineering review. Following that review, the mechanical package accounted for roughly 25% of the total comments across the three MEP trades.

Tools & Applicable Project Criteria

  • Revit: mechanical and plumbing drawings, BIM/model development, schedules, detail sheets, and design documentation.
  • AutoCAD: engineering drawings and documentation.
  • Trace 700: heating and cooling load calculations.
  • Microsoft Excel: pressure-loss and supporting engineering calculations.
  • Microsoft Teams / Microsoft 365: coordination and communication.

Applicable criteria for the Army assignment included UFC 3-410-01 — Heating, Ventilating, and Air Conditioning Systems, along with applicable general mechanical design/documentation requirements under UFC/UFS 3-401-01. These were part of the project’s governing requirements.
