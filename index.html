04 / CAD & Simulation

Needle-Nose Pliers — CAD & Structural Simulation

SolidWorks · Static FEA · Modal analysis

Modeled and assembled a set of needle-nose pliers in SolidWorks from a provided engineering drawing, then evaluated the assembly through static FEA and modal analysis. A 40 psi handle pressure produced the highest stresses near the pivot region, while the frequency study identified the assembly’s natural frequencies and associated mode shapes.
